Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Last updated: May 21, 2026

The Mart is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use and safeguard your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use The Mart website, how we use it, how we protect it and the choices available to you. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not access or use our Website.

1. What Personal Data We Collect

  • Contact Information – name, email address, telephone number and mailing address.
  • Order and shopping information – products, cart activity and information related to services requested.
  • Account Data – information tied to registered user accounts, if applicable.
  • Technical Information – IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system and browsing activity.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies – data about site usage, analytics and preferences.
  • Communications – records of inquiries, support requests and feedback.
  • Email Interconnectivity – information about email opens or links clicked where such technology is used.
  • Media Submissions – uploaded images or media and associated metadata when voluntarily provided.
  • Sensitive Information – only when expressly and voluntarily provided and relevant to a request.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to provide and manage requested services, process orders, administer accounts, respond to inquiries, improve website functionality, personalize content, send updates where permitted, detect fraud or misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We may use essential, functional and analytics cookies. Third-party services embedded in the Website may also set cookies according to their own policies. Browser settings can be used to control cookies, although disabling essential cookies may affect functionality.

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We may share data with service providers that help us operate the Website or fulfil legitimate business functions, and where disclosure is required by law. The Mart does not sell your personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for applicable business, security, accounting or legal requirements. Retention periods can differ depending on the type of record.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls and appropriate security practices. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Website is not directed toward children under 18 and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Links

The Website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and The Mart is not responsible for those independent practices.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
