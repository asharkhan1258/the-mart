Last updated: May 21, 2026

Japkirat Food Mart LLC is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use and safeguard your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Japkirat Food Mart LLC website, how we use it, how we protect it and the choices available to you. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not access or use our Website.

  • Contact Information – name, email address, telephone number and mailing address.
  • Order and shopping information – products, cart activity and information related to services requested.
  • Account Data – information tied to registered user accounts, if applicable.
  • Technical Information – IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system and browsing activity.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies – data about site usage, analytics and preferences.
  • Communications – records of inquiries, support requests and feedback.

We may use information to provide and manage requested services, process orders, administer accounts, respond to inquiries, improve website functionality, personalize content, send updates where permitted, detect fraud or misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls and appropriate security practices. No online system can guarantee absolute security.
